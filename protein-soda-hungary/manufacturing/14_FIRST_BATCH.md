## 1. WHY 1,700 CANS AND NOT MORE, AND NOT LESS

**Not less,** because ~1,700 is what one pallet of 330 ml cans physically is. `DERIVED`: a 330 ml sleek can is ~53–58 mm diameter; a 1200×800 Euro pallet takes ~280 cans/layer × 6 layers ≈ **1,680 cans**; a 1200×1000 industrial pallet ~350–400/layer × 5–6 layers ≈ 1,750–2,400. **Range 1,500–2,500. This is arithmetic, not a quote — the first thing Opre' must confirm.**

**Not more, for a regulatory reason that is irreversible at the artwork stage.**

> ### ⚠️ THE 5,000-CAN CEILING
> Below roughly **5,000 units of 0.1–3 L beverage packaging placed on the Hungarian market in a calendar year**, the DRS/MOHU deposit-return system very probably does not apply to you: **no 50 HUF deposit, no MOHU product registration, no 45-day pre-launch waiting period, NO DRS MARK ON THE ARTWORK, and no deposit float tying up cash.** Above it, DRS is **mandatory**.
>
> **`LIKELY — NEEDS PROFESSIONAL CONFIRMATION.` This must be settled by a Hungarian food-law opinion BEFORE artwork goes to print.**
>
> **Why it is irreversible: a sleeve printed without the DRS mark can never legally carry you past 5,000 units.** You would scrap the sleeves. **Decide before you print, not after.**

At 1,700 cans you sit at **34% of that ceiling** — enough headroom for a second small run in the same year if the first sells out.

**Levy stack at this volume is trivial: ≈5 HUF/can total (EUR 0.014)** — EPR ~2.6 HUF on the can + ~2.52 HUF on the shipper carton. NETA 0 HUF for a zero-sugar, caffeine-free protein soda `LIKELY`. Környezetvédelmi termékdíj abolished 1 Jan 2025 `LIKELY`. DRS deposit 0 under the threshold. **Hungarian product taxation is not your obstacle at this scale. Do not let it distract you.**

⚠️ **And do not use taurine, ever.** It reportedly pushes NETA to ~128.70 HUF/can — EUR 0.35 of pure tax, which alone would exceed your gross margin.

### 2.3 ⚠️ THE BLUNT VERDICT ON THE CAPITAL

**Available: EUR 1,000 personal + ~EUR 2,000 family = EUR 3,000 = 1,089,000 HUF.**
**Base case needs ~EUR 3,250. The gap is ~EUR 250 (≈91,000 HUF).**

**EUR 1,000 alone does not buy a can run. It never did.** The batch only fits inside EUR 3,000 if:

1. **artwork is self-designed** (Canva/Figma) — saves EUR 80–400;
2. **you skip the card reader** and take cash — saves EUR 39;
3. **you negotiate the classification opinion down to a scoped question** rather than a full opinion — saves ~EUR 125;
4. **the contingency is funded from the first EUR 400 of sales rather than held up front** — the riskiest of the four, and the one to avoid if you can.

**Do 1–3 and hold the contingency: EUR 3,000 covers it, just.** Do not spend the contingency on a bigger batch.

⚠️ **Two ingredient warnings that could break this budget:**
- **Whey protein isolate at 118 HUF/can is the second-largest single line and the most volatile.** European producers were reported **SOLD OUT in April 2026**, with WPC80 at EUR 20,250/t. **Get a written WPI price and availability before you commit to ARCH F.** If WPI has moved, the collagen-only fallback formula is your protection — have it costed now, not later.
- **The setup/changeover fee (70 HUF/can amortised, EUR 328 on the batch) is a QUOTE REQUIRED figure that could be double.** At 1,700 cans a fixed fee is punishing: every EUR 100 of setup fee is **21 HUF on every can.** Ask for it as a number, not a percentage.

### 2.4 Lab cost: budget 0, and here is why that is legitimate

**Nutrition analysis is NOT required. This saves EUR 800–2,000.** Reg. (EU) 1169/2011 Art. 31(4) permits nutrition declaration values to be established **by calculation from known average values of the ingredients used.** Your formula is a defined recipe of purchased ingredients with supplier specification sheets — calculate the panel from those specs. `LIKELY — confirm in the food-law classification opinion.`

**Microbiological testing** should sit inside the co-packer's own QA and arrive as a **Certificate of Analysis with the batch**. Private Label Drink publishes exactly that commitment. **Make a per-batch CoA a written condition of the order.** If a co-packer will not provide one, budget for a lab and re-rank them.

**If you have any money left over, spend it here instead:** one **paid advisory hour** with a beverage technologist on protein clarity and can-lacquer compatibility at pH 3.2 — Campden BRI Magyarország and Drink Creator (the &T Tonic team) are the two named leads. **One hour may prevent a failed 1,700-can run.**

| Scenario | Contribution/can | Verdict |
|---|---:|---|

> ### ⚠️ WHOLESALE IS NOT VIABLE UNTIL ~25,000 CANS
> COGS falls to ~420 HUF at 25,000 cans, the first volume at which a 470 HUF wholesale price produces a positive **+50 HUF**. At 5,000 cans COGS is ~699 HUF (−47% gross margin at wholesale). At 10,000 cans ~556 HUF (still −86 HUF). **Anyone who offers to "take a pallet at wholesale" in month one is offering to take your money. Say no politely and offer consignment instead.**

### 3.1 Break-even, stated plainly

- **Total batch cost: 834,500 HUF.**
- **Net revenue per can sold at 799: 629 HUF.**
- **BREAK-EVEN = 834,500 ÷ 629 = 1,327 cans.** `DERIVED`

Budget **~175 cans as free samples** (your main sales tool — this is deliberate, not waste). That leaves **1,525 sellable cans**, of which you must sell **1,327 = 87%** just to get your money back.

| RRP | Cans sold (of 1,525) | Net position on the batch |
|---|---|---|
| 799 HUF | all 1,525 | **+124,500 HUF (+EUR 343)** |
| 899 HUF | all 1,525 | **+244,800 HUF (+EUR 674)** |
| **699 HUF** | all 1,525 | **+4,600 HUF (+EUR 13)** — **essentially zero. This is why 699 is the floor, not the target.** |
| 799 HUF | 1,327 | **0 — break-even** |
| 799 HUF | 1,000 | **−205,500 HUF (−EUR 566)** |

**The honest read: batch 1 is a paid education, not a profit centre.** If everything goes right you end the batch with roughly your money back plus ~EUR 340 and — far more valuable — a real product, real stockists and real quotes. **Plan for that outcome, not for a windfall.**

**Open question for your accountant:** this model assumes you charge 27% ÁFA. Whether your chosen legal structure is VAT-registered or VAT-exempt materially changes the 629 HUF net-revenue line. **Ask before you set the price list.**
