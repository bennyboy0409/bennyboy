## 1. THE PHYSICAL PROBLEM

### 1.1 Space actually required

| Configuration | Footprint | Height | Practical space incl. access |
|---|---|---|---|
| **On the pallet, as delivered** (71 trays, 8 layers) | **0.96 m²** (1200 × 800 mm) | ~1,066 mm | **~2 m²** |
| **De-palletised, cases stacked 6 high** | **1.27 m²** (12 stacks of ~0.106 m²) | **720 mm** | **~2.5 m²** |
| De-palletised, stacked 4 high | 1.90 m² | 480 mm | ~3.5 m² |
| Shelved (2 shelves × ~0.65 m²) | 0.65 m² | shelf height | ~1.5 m² |

`DERIVED — case footprint 265 × 400 mm = 0.106 m²; 71 cases ÷ 6 per stack = 12 stacks. Case height ~120 mm.`

**Stacking 6 cases high is structurally safe:** the bottom tray carries 42.5 kg spread across 24 cans = 1.8 kg per can. A filled aluminium can takes tens of kg axially — that is how pallets stack 15 layers. `ESTIMATE — derivation shown.` **Keep stacks aligned can-over-can; a misaligned stack crushes the tray board, not the cans.**

### 1.2 Floor loading — check this if you are above ground level

| Configuration | Load |
|---|---|
| Palletised, 629 kg over 0.96 m² | **~655 kg/m²** |
| De-palletised, 6 high, 603 kg over 1.27 m² | **~475 kg/m²** |
| De-palletised, 4 high, over 1.90 m² | **~317 kg/m²** |
| Spread over 4 m² | **~151 kg/m²** |

Typical residential floor design loads are in the **150–200 kg/m²** region. `ESTIMATE — MODEL KNOWLEDGE, NOT VERIFIED. Confirm with a builder or the building's documentation if you are storing above ground floor.`

**Rules that follow:**
- **Ground floor on a concrete slab, or a garage floor: no issue at any configuration.**
- **Upper-floor flat: do not leave it palletised and do not build a single tall block.** Spread the cases along a **load-bearing wall** across 3–4 m², stacked no more than 4 high. Never in the middle of a room span.
- **Never store it on a balcony** — load, temperature swing and rain all fail at once.

### 1.3 Getting it in — the delivery day plan

A 629 kg pallet cannot be moved by a person, and you do not own a pallet truck.

| Step | What must be true | If it isn't |
|---|---|---|
| Booking | **Specify "emelőhátfalas gépkocsi" (tail-lift) and a residential/no-dock delivery** | Truck arrives with no tail-lift → **failed delivery + re-delivery fee** |
| Arrival | Level, hard, ground-level standing space for the truck to lower the pallet | Driver refuses to unload |
| Unload | **You break the pallet at the tailgate and carry 71 cases in by hand** | 45–75 min, two people, 8.5 kg per trip |
| Pallet disposal | The empty EPAL pallet is yours | Keep it — you will want stock off the floor (§5.1) |

`ESTIMATE — 71 cases at ~40–60 seconds per case per person.`

**Do NOT plan on the driver helping, waiting, or carrying anything.** Book a two-hour window, have a second person present, and clear the route beforehand.

**Practical alternative worth asking for: "Tudnak rekeszenként, raklap nélkül is szállítani?"** Some carriers will deliver a small consignment as loose cases rather than a pallet. If yes, the tail-lift problem disappears entirely.

## 3. HACCP / GHP FOR A STORAGE-ONLY OPERATION

**You do need one. There is no blanket exemption for distributors** (Reg. 852/2004 Art. 5(1) applies at all stages after primary production). `LIKELY — NOT VERIFIED.`

**But it is proportionate, and it is free.** The intended low-cost route for exactly this business is:
- Adopt and **adapt** Hungary's **national GHP guide for *kereskedelem*** (Jó Higiéniai Gyakorlat útmutató), expected free on `portal.nebih.gov.hu` — **exact URL UNKNOWN, must be located**;
- plus a **simplified hazard-analysis document**, not a full 7-principle HACCP plan. A sealed, shelf-stable can under ambient storage has **few or arguably no critical control points** at the distribution stage.

**Consultant cost: UNKNOWN — QUOTE REQUIRED. Self-drafting from the free national guide is a legitimate route for a storage-only operation and is what your budget implies. Budget 0 HUF and a weekend.**

**What your document must contain** (drafting checklist, derived from Annex II of 852/2004 — not a verified regulatory list):

1. Business identification, FELIR number, establishment address, scope, responsible person
2. Product description: 330 ml hermetically sealed aluminium can, ambient shelf-stable, pH ~3.2 and thermal process per co-packer spec, **allergen: MILK (whey)**, declared shelf life
3. Flow diagram: co-packer → inbound transport → goods-in check → storage → picking → outbound transport → customer
4. Hazard analysis. For sealed cans the real hazards are **physical damage (seam/can integrity), temperature abuse, pest activity on packaging, contamination from co-stored non-food items, and stock-rotation failure (expired product sold)** — not microbial growth inside the sealed can
5. **Goods-in acceptance criteria**: lot number recorded, best-before recorded, dented/leaking cans rejected, vehicle condition noted
6. Storage controls: temperature range, off-floor, segregation from chemicals, **FEFO rotation**
7. Pest control arrangements
8. Cleaning schedule
9. Personal hygiene and training records (yes, even for one person)
10. Traceability procedure + withdrawal/recall procedure, as annexes
11. Non-conforming product handling: quarantine area, disposal records
12. Record retention rules and review date

**The three most likely adverse findings at an inspection of this exact business, in order:** (1) traceability records that cannot link an inbound lot to an outbound customer; (2) **a garage that co-stores food with chemicals or cannot hold temperature**; (3) a label detail. `ESTIMATE — A3's judgement, not a statistic.` **All three are preventable at zero cost.**

## 5. PRACTICAL HOME-STORAGE CHECKLIST

### 5.1 Set-up — do these before the pallet arrives

- [ ] **Confirm G1/G2 by phone with the járási hivatal** (§2.3). Nothing below matters if the answer is no.
- [ ] **Check the deed / *társasházi SZMSZ* / landlord** permits commercial storage at the address (G4).
- [ ] **Choose a heated, dark, interior room.** Not a garage, not a balcony, not an unheated cellar. **See §5.2.**
- [ ] **Ground floor if possible.** If upper floor, spread cases along a load-bearing wall, max 4 high (§1.2).
- [ ] **Get stock off the floor** — keep the delivery pallet, or use shelving, or lay down battens. **Never cases directly on concrete** (condensation → rust on the can base, mould on the tray board).
- [ ] **Clear a hard, level, ground-level standing space for the tail-lift truck.** Confirm the address is reachable by a truck.
- [ ] **Line up a second person for delivery day** and a two-hour window.
- [ ] **Buy nothing.** No pallet truck (EUR 250–400 and unnecessary once the pallet is broken), no racking, no shelving unless you already own it.

### 5.2 🔴 Temperature — the one that can destroy the whole batch

| Risk | Consequence | Rule |
|---|---|---|
| **Below 0 °C** | Water expands ~9 % on freezing. **A frozen can deforms or bursts its seam.** An unheated Hungarian garage in January is a **total-loss risk on 834,900 HUF of inventory.** | **NEVER store below ~4 °C. This alone disqualifies an unheated garage or outbuilding.** |
| **Above ~30 °C** | Protein haze, sedimentation, Maillard browning, off-flavour at pH 3.2. **Exceeding the co-packer's validated storage range voids the shelf life printed on your can — which is YOUR legal statement under FIC Art. 8(1).** | **Keep under 25 °C. A closed Hungarian garage or attic exceeds 40 °C in July.** |
| **Direct light** | Sleeve fade, accelerated degradation | Store dark or covered |
| **Damp (cellar)** | Can-base corrosion, sleeve and tray-board damage | Avoid unventilated cellars |

**Cheapest control: a 2,000–4,000 HUF min/max thermometer in the storage room, read and logged weekly.** `ESTIMATE — retail price band, NOT VERIFIED.` **That log is also exactly what an inspector wants to see.** It is the highest-value few thousand forints in this file.

**⚠️ ASK THE CO-PACKER, BEFORE ORDERING: "Mennyi a szavatossági idő, és milyen tárolási hőmérséklet-tartományra érvényes a stabilitási adat?"** Then check it against your realistic sell-through: **1,704 cans over 9 months = 189/month = ~6 cans/day, every day.** If the declared shelf life is shorter than the time it will realistically take you to sell the batch, **you have an inventory write-off built into the plan before you start.** `UNKNOWN — QUOTE REQUIRED. This is the storage question with the largest money attached.`

### 5.3 Ongoing — the inspector's list

- [ ] **Segregate absolutely from all non-food items** — chemicals, fuel, paint, garden products, tyres, cleaning supplies, pet food. **Cross-contamination from co-stored non-food goods is the most likely adverse finding, and it is the specific reason a garage is a weak candidate.**
- [ ] **Nothing stored against a damp wall or under a window.**
- [ ] **Pest control**: no food waste in the room, sealed bin, a visual check each time you pick stock. Note any evidence in the log.
- [ ] **Cleaning schedule**, written, weekly, ticked.
- [ ] **FEFO** — first-expired-first-out. With one batch this is trivial; write the rule down anyway.
- [ ] **Stock record from day one**, in a free spreadsheet: date, in/out, quantity, **lot number**, best-before, customer. **This is the first thing an inspector asks for, and it is your only traceability defence.**
- [ ] **Goods-in record on delivery day**: lot number, best-before, number of cases received, number of damaged/dented cans rejected, vehicle condition. **Photograph the pallet before you break it.**
- [ ] **Quarantine corner** for dented, leaking or returned cans, physically separate and labelled. **A dented can is not sellable and must not re-enter stock.**
- [ ] **Damaged-can procedure**, written: quarantine → record → dispose → log.
- [ ] **Weekly temperature log** (§5.2).

### 5.4 What you do NOT have to deal with

- **No deposit returns, no empties, no reverse logistics, no deposit float.** Below ~5,000 cans/year the DRS/MOHU deposit system very probably does not apply. **Your storage holds forward stock only.** `LIKELY — NEEDS PROFESSIONAL CONFIRMATION. Keep year-one volume under ~5,000 cans and this stays true.`
- **No refrigeration.** The product is ambient shelf-stable. **Do not let a gym or a store tell you it needs chilled storage** — chilled *display* is a sales choice, not a storage requirement.
- **No racking, no forklift, no pallet truck.** Once the pallet is broken into cases, everything is hand-handled.
