**1. Can this legally be sold from Hungary?**
**Yes.** As a brand owner/distributor you register a food business activity with NÉBIH, obtain a FELIR identifier (free), and place a compliant Hungarian label on the can. No product pre-approval is required for a normal foodstuff. → `legal/08_HUNGARY_FOOD_COMPLIANCE.md`

**2. What exact company/setup is required?**
Cheapest: **add TEÁOR codes 4634 (ital nagykereskedelem) + 4725 (ital-kiskereskedelem) to an existing adult-owned family company** (€0–165). Fallback: father as a **secondary-occupation egyéni vállalkozó** (0 HUF registration + 5,000 HUF/yr chamber). **Do not** register a full-time EV — 101,682 HUF/month in contributions at zero revenue. → `legal/07_HUNGARY_BUSINESS_SETUP.md`

**3. Can the founder personally operate it, or must an adult?**
**An adult must.** Under 18 you cannot be an *egyéni vállalkozó* (Evectv. 2009. évi CXV. 3. § (2)) and cannot be an *ügyvezető* (**Ptk. 3:22 § (1)** — *"Vezető tisztségviselő az a nagykorú személy lehet…"*). No parental consent cures either. You **can** be a Kft. *tag* through your *törvényes képviselő*, and an employee from 16, freely disposing of income you earn (Ptk. 2:12 § (2) c). → `legal/07_HUNGARY_BUSINESS_SETUP.md`

**4. Is 20 g protein / 330 ml technically realistic?**
**Yes, but it is the ceiling, not a starting point.** Five independent formulators converged on 20 g per **355 ml**; your 330 ml is 60.6 g/L — ~7.6% more concentrated. It works only because the *whey* fraction sits at 30.3 g/L, inside the safe corridor. All-whey at 60.6 g/L does not. → `product/05_FOOD_CHEMISTRY.md`

**5. What formulation architecture?**
**ARCH F:** 10 g hydrolysed bovine collagen + 10 g whey protein isolate + 1.7 g L-leucine + 150 mg L-tryptophan = **20 g declared protein**, ~213 HUF of ingredients. The amino-acid spike costs 10.31 HUF and enables a "3 g leucine per can" claim that neutralises the collagen objection. → `product/03_PRODUCT_ARCHITECTURE.md`

**6. One SKU or two?**
**One.** Two SKUs split a 1,700-can run into two unsellable halves, need two GTINs, two artworks and two minimum ingredient buys — and the caffeine version triggers the mandatory caffeine warning plus unresolved NETA tax exposure. → `01_FINAL_RECOMMENDATION.md`

**22. What lab work before sale?**
**Legally, none.** Reg. 1169/2011 **Art. 31(4)** permits nutrition values to be established by **calculation** from ingredient values — saving €800–2,000. Demand a **Certificate of Analysis per batch** from the co-packer instead (usually inside the tolling fee).
**One exception worth paying for:** a finished-can nitrogen analysis (~€100–150) before making "20 g protein" a front-of-pack claim, because half the protein is collagen and a nitrogen-conversion-factor mismatch is the most likely way that number gets disproved. → `product/06_SAFETY_AND_SHELF_LIFE.md`
