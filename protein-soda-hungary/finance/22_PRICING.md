## GYM WHOLESALE SCENARIOS — not before ~10,000 cans

### The margin arithmetic everyone gets wrong

A retailer's gross margin is **(net selling price − net buying price) ÷ net selling price**. Both sides must be net of ÁFA. Comparing a VAT-inclusive shelf price against a VAT-exclusive wholesale price **overstates the gym's real margin by roughly 16 percentage points** — and an earlier version of this table did exactly that.

| Gym buys at (net) | Gym sells at (RRP) | Gym's net selling price | **Gym's REAL margin** | *(as previously mis-stated)* |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 430 | 799 | 629.13 | **31.7%** | ~~46%~~ |
| 470 | 799 | 629.13 | **25.3%** | ~~41%~~ |
| 470 | 899 | 707.87 | **33.6%** | ~~47%~~ |
| 520 | 899 | 707.87 | **26.5%** | ~~42%~~ |
| 520 | 799 | 629.13 | **17.3%** | ~~35%~~ |

**Not one of those rows clears the 35–45% a Hungarian drinks retailer expects `LIKELY — not sourced to a trade publication`.** Every wholesale scenario in the old plan looked acceptable on paper and would have been refused in the room.

### What a real trade margin actually costs

| Gym's target margin | Required wholesale price @ **899** RRP | @ **799** RRP |
|---|---|---|
| 35% | **460 HUF** | 409 HUF |
| 40% | **425 HUF** | 377 HUF |
| 45% | **389 HUF** | 346 HUF |

Now put your cost next to it:

| Your volume | COGS | Contribution at 425 net (gym gets 40% @ 899) | Contribution at 460 net (gym gets 35% @ 899) |
|---|---|---|---|
| **1,700** | 524 | **−99** ❌ | **−64** ❌ |
| 5,000 | 431 | −6 ❌ | +29 ⚠️ |
| **10,000** | **390** | **+35** ✅ | **+70** ✅ |
| 25,000 | 349 | **+76** ✅ | **+111** ✅ |
| 50,000 | 319 | +106 ✅ | +141 ✅ |

**Conclusion: a genuine 35–45% trade margin becomes affordable at ~10,000 cans — and only at a 899 RRP.** At 799 RRP the same 40% margin implies a 377 HUF wholesale price, which you cannot reach until ~25,000 cans.

> **Recommended wholesale, from ~10,000 cans: 425–470 HUF net at 899 RRP.** Below 10,000 cans, do not quote a trade price to anyone. Once a number is in writing it cannot be withdrawn without losing the account.
