## WHY BATCH 1 CANNOT BE WHOLESALED

| **Margin** | **−21** ❌ |

| Sold direct at 799 | HUF/can |
|---|---|
| Consumer pays | 799 |
| less 27% ÁFA | −170 |
| Net revenue | 629 |
| less COGS | −491 |
| **Contribution** | **+138** ✅ |

**Batch 1 must be sold where you keep the retail margin:**

1. **Consignment into gym fridges** — you own the stock, gym takes 20–30% per can sold. Best option: no cash risk for the gym, so it is an easy yes.
2. **Direct sales** at gyms, competitions, university sport centres
3. **D2C** in multi-packs only (single-can shipping is economically absurd — see `logistics/23_SHIPPING.md`)

**Wholesale becomes viable at ~25,000 cans (COGS ~420 HUF → +50 HUF margin at 470).**

## CONSIGNMENT MODEL — the recommended batch 1 offer

| Term | Value |
|---|---|
| Gym pays upfront | **0 HUF** |
| RRP | 799 HUF |
| Gym keeps per can sold | **200 HUF (25%)** |
| You receive | 599 HUF gross = **471 net of VAT** |
| Your contribution | **−20 HUF** ⚠️ |

> ⚠️ **At a 25% consignment cut, batch 1 breaks even at best.** Use **20% (160 HUF to the gym)** → you net 511 HUF → **+20 HUF/can**. Thin but positive.

**Better still: mix the channels.** Target ~60% direct sales (full 138 HUF contribution) and ~40% consignment (+20 HUF). Blended ≈ **+91 HUF/can** ≈ **€425 on the batch**.
