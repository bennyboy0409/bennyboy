# 20 — CAPITAL SCENARIOS

**How much money do you actually need, and what does each amount buy?**
1 EUR = 363 HUF · All figures `ESTIMATE` unless tagged otherwise

---

## THE HEADLINE

> **€1,000 does not buy 330 ml cans.**
> **€2,000–2,600 buys ~1,700 real, sellable, professionally filled cans.**
> **€7,500–10,000 is the WORST place to be — avoid it entirely.**

---

## ⚠️ THE DEAD ZONE — the most important finding in this file

There is a **capital dead zone between roughly €6,000 and €20,000**. Spending in it makes you *worse off* than spending less.

**Why:** at ~5,000 cans/year you cross the Hungarian DRS threshold. That triggers:

- MOHU producer + per-SKU registration
- A 45-day pre-launch gate
- **The DRS mark permanently printed on the can artwork**
- A deposit working-capital float of 50 HUF × every can in the channel
- Gyms having to charge deposits and handle empties — real listing friction

…but you still have **none of the scale economics** (COGS is still 550–700 HUF against a 470 HUF wholesale price).

**You take on all the compliance burden and none of the margin.**

| Zone | Cans | Verdict |
|---|---|---|
| **€2,000–3,000** | 1,400–2,000 | ✅ **Sub-DRS. Validate cheaply.** |
| €3,000–6,000 | 2,000–4,500 | ✅ Still sub-DRS. Acceptable if LOIs justify it |
| **€6,000–20,000** | 5,000–15,000 | ❌ **DEAD ZONE. All the burden, none of the margin.** |
| €25,000+ | 25,000+ | ✅ Wholesale becomes viable at 420 HUF COGS |

**Practical rule: stay under 5,000 cans until you can jump straight to 25,000+.**

## SCENARIO: €1,000 (founder's own capital only)

**Can you legally produce and sell cans? NO.**

**What €1,000 does buy — and it is not nothing:**

| Item | Cost |
|---|---|
| Business setup (add TEÁOR to family company) | €0–165 |
| Chamber contribution | €14 |
| Food business registration + FELIR | €0 |
| **Hungarian food-law classification opinion** | €150–400 |
| Benchmark products for tasting | €25 |
| Domain + one-page site | €45 |
| Gym sampling campaign (see below) | €0 |
| **Reserve** | ~€350–750 |

**Milestone reached:** a legally constituted food business, all three make-or-break legal questions answered (DRS / ÁFA / NETA), written co-packer quotes in hand, and signed LOIs from gyms.

**That is the correct use of €1,000.** It buys certainty, and certainty is what lets your father decide rationally.

### The alternative €1,000 route: launch a powder SKU first

~1,000 stick-packs / sachets for **€700–1,500**. This is the **only** route that puts a real, sellable, branded product in your hands for under €1,000. It de-risks everything downstream: you learn the flavour, build the gym relationships, register the business, and test whether anyone actually buys.

**Verdict:** worth serious consideration. It is not the product you want, but it is a real business, and it makes the can order far less speculative.

## THE SMALLEST SENSIBLE AMOUNT

> ## **€3,000 total — of which €2,000 from your father.**

**Why not less:** €2,000 leaves no contingency, and first production runs go wrong.

**Why not more:** every euro above €3,000 buys cans you have not yet proven you can sell, and above ~€6,000 you cross into the DRS dead zone where compliance burden arrives before margin does.

**What the €2,000 buys your father:** a real product, in real gyms, with real sell-through data, inside 90 days — for less than the price of a used phone. If it works, the case for €30,000 writes itself. If it doesn't, he has lost €2,000 and you have learned more than any business course teaches.

## THE ESCALATION LADDER

| Stage | Trigger | Capital | Cans | COGS |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **0** | Today | €1,000 (yours) | 0 | Legal setup + LOIs + quotes |
| **1** | 8+ signed LOIs | +€2,000 (father) | 1,700 | 491 HUF |
| **2** | Batch 1 sold in <90 days, 3+ repeat orders | +€5,000 | 3,200 | 465 HUF |
| **3** | Chain agreement OR 2 sold-out batches | +€28,000 | 25,000 | 420 HUF |
| **4** | Distributor or national listing | +€84,000 | 100,000 | 304 HUF |

**Never skip a stage.** Each one is priced so that failure is survivable.

> **Stage 3 is where the business becomes real** — it is the first point at which wholesale to gyms is profitable, which is the only way to sell volume without personally handling every can.
