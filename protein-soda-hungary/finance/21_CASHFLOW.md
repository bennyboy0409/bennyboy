## ⚠️ THE DANGER POINT IS WEEK 11–12

**Minimum cash balance: ~€177.**

At that moment you own 1,700 cans and almost no money. Every euro of your capital is sitting in aluminium in your garage.

**If anything goes wrong here — a rejected batch, a freight surcharge, a gym that changes its mind — you have no room to react.**

### Three ways to protect week 11

1. **Negotiate 30% deposit instead of 50%.** Ask explicitly. Worst case they say no.
2. **Pre-sell before delivery.** Take deposits from gyms in weeks 7–10 while production runs. Even 300 cans pre-paid at 599 HUF = **€494** — it triples your week-11 buffer.
3. **Hold €350 contingency untouched.** It is not spending money. It is the difference between a setback and a failure.

## THE SCENARIO WHERE YOU ARE "PROFITABLE" AND STILL FAIL

This is the trap. Read it twice.

You sell **1,700 cans wholesale to 5 gyms at 470 HUF net, on 30-day terms.**

- On paper: revenue €2,200, COGS €2,300 → **already a loss of €100**
- In cash: you paid €2,300 in week 11 and receive nothing until week 15+
- If one gym pays late or returns unsold stock, you cannot fund anything else

**Meanwhile the same 1,700 cans sold direct at 799 HUF produce €2,944 and cash arrives weekly.**

> **The channel decision is a cashflow decision, not just a margin decision.** Direct sales pay you immediately. Wholesale pays you in 30 days at a price that loses money at this volume.

---

## PAYMENT TERMS TO INSIST ON

| Counterparty | Terms to seek |
|---|---|
| **Co-packer** | 30% deposit / 70% on delivery. Never 100% upfront |
| **Gyms (consignment)** | Settle every 2 weeks, or on restock |
| **Gyms (wholesale, later)** | **Prepayment or 8 days** for the first 3 orders. 30 days only after they have reordered twice |
| **Direct/D2C** | Payment before dispatch, always |

> **Never extend 30-day credit to a new gym on your first order.** You cannot afford to finance someone else's business.

## WHAT HAPPENS IF THE BATCH DOES NOT SELL

Honest planning for the likely-enough case.

| Sold in 6 months | Cash recovered | Net position |
|---|---|---|
| 1,700 (100%) | €2,944 | **+€644** |
| 1,190 (70%) | €2,061 | −€239 |
| 850 (50%) | €1,472 | **−€828** |
| 425 (25%) | €736 | **−€1,564** |

**Cans do not expire quickly** — a realistic best-before is 9–12 months `ESTIMATE`, so slow sales are recoverable if you keep selling. But note the asymmetry: **selling half your batch loses €828, while selling all of it makes €644.**

This asymmetry is the entire argument for getting LOIs before you order.
