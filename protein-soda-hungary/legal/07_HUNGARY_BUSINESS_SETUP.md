### 1.2 The gyámhatóság question — the only thing that can add 4–6 weeks

**Open question:** does the gyámhatóság (járási hivatal gyámügyi osztály) have to approve a minor **acquiring** a Kft. üzletrész at founding, when the money is a parent's gift?

- Sources agree approval is needed to **dispose of or encumber** a minor's existing üzletrész above a threshold.
- The threshold is reported **inconsistently**: some sources say **company shares / üzletrész / értékpapír above 100,000 HUF (EUR 276)**; others say **ten times the minimum old-age pension**. Gyer. **149/1997. (IX. 10.) Korm. r.** (≈ 25. §), https://njt.jog.gov.hu/jogszabaly/1997-149-20-22 ; OTP guidance https://www.otpbank.hu/static/portal/sw/file/Tajekoztato_kiskoru_vagyonanak_terh_adott_megb_20180706.pdf
- **Status: NEEDS PROFESSIONAL CONFIRMATION.** Assume it **may** be required for a 3,000,000 HUF Kft. stake. **Budget 4–6 weeks if it is.**
- If parent and minor are on **opposing sides** of a transaction, an **eseti gyám (ad hoc guardian)** must be appointed. https://drfeketelegal.hu/uncategorized-hu/kiskoru-vagyonszerzese-mikor-kell-gyamhivatali-jovahagyas-es-mikor-szukseges-eseti-gyam/ — SEARCH-VERIFIED

> **This is a reason not to found a Kft. for batch 1.** The recommended route (existing family company, you as employee) does not touch the gyámhatóság at all.

### 1.4 What you CAN do at your age, without a parent in the room

- Hold a **DÁP digital identity from age 14** (with a személyigazolvány) — https://dap.gov.hu/ — SEARCH-VERIFIED (https://www.vallalkozas-okosan.hu/ugyfelkapu_dap_digitalis_allampolgar). **Digital identity is NOT legal capacity.** It lets you log into portals; it does not let you be an EV or an ügyvezető.
- **Receive and spend your wages** from the venture (Ptk. 2:12 § (2) c)).
- Do all the operational work: formulation briefs, supplier emails, gym visits, design, social, sampling. None of that requires legal capacity.

### 2.2 TWELVE-MONTH CASH COST, ZERO REVENUE — the table that decides it

**ESTIMATE. Assumptions: no premises, no payroll beyond the minor, brand owner buying finished cans from a Hungarian co-packer. Accounting taken at the bottom of published ranges. Székhelyszolgáltatás 5,800 + 27% ÁFA = 7,366 HUF/mo × 12 = 88,392 HUF. Lawyer 36,000 + 27% ÁFA = 45,720 HUF.**

| **② New EV, SECONDARY occupation** (father has a ≥36 h/week job) | 0 / 0 | 5,000 chamber + 180,000 accounting (15,000×12); contributions ≈ **0** | **≈ 185,000** | **≈ 510** | **unlimited personal** | ✓ employee |

**Why ③ is a trap (DERIVED, arithmetic shown).** Minimálbér 2026 = **322,800 HUF/month** (SEARCH-VERIFIED; decree cited variously as 394/2024. (XII.12.) or 426/2025. (XII.23.) Korm. r. — **verify in Magyar Közlöny**). From 2026-01-01 the szocho minimum base for a főfoglalkozású EV fell from 112.5% to **100%** of the minimum wage (NAV, https://nav.gov.hu/ado/szocialis_hozzajarulasi_ado/Csokken_a_szocialis_hozzajarulasi_ado_alapja_es_valtozik_az_egyeni_vallalkozok_bevallasi_gyakorisaga — CONFIRMED).
- TB járulék 18.5% × 322,800 = **59,718 HUF**
- Szocho 13% × 322,800 = **41,964 HUF**
- **Total 101,682 HUF/month = EUR 280/month = 1,220,184 HUF/yr = EUR 3,362/yr — payable with zero revenue.**
- That alone consumes your entire EUR 3,000 in **under 11 months**, and your personal EUR 1,000 in **3.5 months**.

**Secondary-occupation EV (② — the fallback).** If the father already holds a **≥36 h/week employment** (or is a full-time student), there is **NO minimum contribution base**; 18.5% + 13% apply only to actual taxable income, quarterly. Sources: https://www.onadozo.hu/hirek/atalanyado-egyeni-vallalkozo-jarulekfizetese-heti-36-oras-munkaviszony-mellett-11628 ; https://accountiq.hu/blog/masodallasu-egyeni-vallalkozo-inditasa-adozasa — SEARCH-VERIFIED. **This is the single most decisive fact in the whole file.**

**Kft. "zero-contribution" configuration.** If the ügyvezető is **not a member** and serves under an **unpaid megbízási jogviszony**, no contribution liability arises. If the ügyvezető **is** a member, they are a *társas vállalkozó* and the minimum-base rules bite **unless** they hold a ≥36 h/week job elsewhere. Sources: https://adozona.hu/tb_jarulekok_nyugdij/kft_ugyvezetoje_tarsas_vallalkozo_ugyvezeto_50PXTH ; NAV info leaflet 12. Status: **NEEDS PROFESSIONAL CONFIRMATION — this is the most commonly mis-set-up item in Hungarian micro-companies and it can silently cost more than everything else combined.**

### 2.3 VERDICT

1. **Use ① — an existing adult-owned family company.** Add TEÁOR **4634** (+ **4725** for direct sales). Near-zero cost, the bank account and probably the food-business registration already exist, and you participate as employee. **This is the launch vehicle for batch 1.**
2. **If there is no family company: ② father as secondary-occupation EV** — ≈ **EUR 510/year**. Only if he genuinely has a ≥36 h/week job.
3. **Migrate to ⑤ a Kft. before the first production run that exceeds ~10,000 cans**, and buy product liability cover at the same time (§11). Not before — your capital cannot fund a Kft.'s first year **and** batch 1.
4. **Never**: minor as EV, minor as ügyvezető, minor as Bt. beltag. All legally impossible.

If the foreign co-packer supplies a **service** (tolling / contract filling, where **you** own the liquid, the cans and the sleeves), the place of supply under **Áfa tv. 37. § (1)** is Hungary. Then:

> *"Más közösségi adóalanytól igénybe vett főszabály szerinti (Áfa tv. 37. § (1) bekezdés) szolgáltatás esetén az **alanyi adómentes adóalanynak a szolgáltatás igénybevételét megelőzően közösségi adószámot kell igényelnie**"* … *"a felszámított áfát … **nem helyezhetik levonásba**."*

**CONFIRMED.** Sources: https://www.saldo.hu/cikk/default/kulfoldrol-igenybe-vett-szolgaltatasok-afakotelezettsege-alanyi-adomentes-adoalanyoknal ; https://www.online-adotanacsadas.hu/alanyi-mentes-adozok-nemzetkozi-szolgaltatas/

**The very first euro** of foreign co-packing *service* forces an alanyi adómentes company to get a közösségi adószám, self-assess 27% with **no deduction**, and file monthly '65 + 'A60 anyway.

> **Bottom line: whichever way a foreign co-packing deal is structured — finished goods over EUR 10,000, or a filling service at any amount — an alanyi adómentes Hungarian company pays 27% Hungarian VAT it can never get back, while still running VAT filings. The exemption buys almost nothing and costs almost everything.**
