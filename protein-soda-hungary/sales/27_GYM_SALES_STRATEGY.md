## THE OFFER — memorise this, it is the whole document

> **"You pay me nothing. I put a case in your fridge. You settle 799 Ft per can you actually sell, every two weeks. Whatever you charge above that is yours — I recommend 899 Ft, so that's 100 Ft a can in your pocket. If it doesn't move in 90 days I take it back at my cost."**

| Term | Value |
|---|---|
| Gym pays upfront | **0 HUF / €0** |
| Model | **Consignment (bizomány)** — you keep title until the can is sold |
| **Recommended RRP** | **899 HUF (€2.48)** |
| **Opening ask — gym keeps** | **100 HUF (€0.28) = 11% of RRP** → settlement to you **799 HUF gross** → **+105.13 HUF/can** |
| **Maximum you may concede** | **160 HUF (€0.44) = 18% of RRP** → settlement **739 HUF gross** → **+57.89 HUF/can** |
| **Break-even gym cut** | **233 HUF (26% of RRP).** `DERIVED: 899 − (524 × 1.27) = 233.52.` Above it you are paying a gym to hold your stock |
| **Floor price** | **899 HUF.** A gym may price ABOVE it; it may not price below it in batch 1 |
| Settlement cycle | **Every 14 days**, in person, physical count |
| First placement | **1–2 cases (24–48 cans)** |
| Consigned stock outstanding, all accounts | **Cap at ~720 cans / 30 cases** — see §5 and `logistics/25_FULFILMENT.md` §6 |

**Do NOT open with "would you like to buy a case?"** At 524 HUF COGS you cannot sell a case profitably to a gym anyway (§3). The zero-risk placement is not a fallback — it is the product.

> **Be honest with yourself about the gym's share.** 100–160 HUF is **11–18% of RRP**, far below the 35–45% a Hungarian retailer expects on stock it *buys*. **The reason it is acceptable is that the gym risks nothing: no cash out, no inventory, no write-off, no dead stock.** You are not selling them a margin. You are selling them a free shelf slot that pays. Say that out loud rather than hoping they don't do the percentage.

Ask, but do not plan against 18%.**

### Six reasons consignment wins at 1,700 cans

1. **Wholesale is loss-making. There is no version of it that works at this batch size.** At 524 HUF COGS a 470 HUF trade price loses 54 HUF/can. The choice is not consignment vs. profitable wholesale — it is consignment vs. no listing.
2. **It removes the gym's only real objection.** An unknown brand at a premium price is inventory risk. Zero cash out = nothing to refuse.
3. **You keep title, so you can move stock.** A dead account's case goes into a live account's fridge next week. Under wholesale it sits in someone else's fridge until it expires.
4. **You get per-account sell-through data.** That is the asset that makes conversation #2 — with a distributor, a chain, or an investor — possible at all (`C7` §8.3). Sell-in data proves nothing; sell-through proves everything.
5. **It is the only structure that fits your cash position.** You cannot finance receivables with €1,000 (`C2` §7, `C8` §4.4, independently).
6. **It caps your downside per account at one case** — 24 cans, **12,576 HUF (€34.6)** of COGS.

### The two real costs of consignment — do not pretend these away

- **You carry the inventory and the risk.** If nothing sells, you own 1,700 unsold cans and the loss is total.
- **It costs founder-hours.** Each account needs a physical visit every 14 days: count, restock, settle, invoice. Budget **30–45 minutes per account per fortnight** including travel. **At 15 accounts that is roughly one full day every two weeks.** That, not market size, is what caps your account count (`C8` §9.5: founder-hours are the binding constraint).

### The signalling risk, and how to defuse it

`C8` §4.4 warns: *"Never offer consignment as your opening terms. It signals you expect the product not to sell."* That warning is correct **when profitable wholesale is available**. It is not, here. Defuse the signal with framing:

- Do **not** call it *bizomány*, *sale-or-return* or *visszárus*. Call it a **"90-day launch placement" (90 napos bevezető kihelyezés)**.
- State the expiry out loud: **"This is how I launch batch 1. Once volume supports it, it becomes a normal wholesale line."** That makes it a launch mechanic, not a weakness. ⚠️ **Do not name a future wholesale price.** At batch-1 volume you cannot honour 470–520 Ft, and a number said out loud in a first meeting becomes the price you are held to. Wholesale becomes contribution-positive at **~10,000 cans** (COGS ~390 HUF), not before.
- Pair it with the exclusivity offer (§11). You are giving something, not begging.

- **Hard stop at 60 cans.** Not 5%, not "about 80" — 60. Count them.

## 7. WHERE THE PROSPECTS COME FROM

### AllYouCanMove and MultiSport — **prospect directories, NOT channels**

`STRUCTURAL ANALYSIS` (`C8` §2.3). **This is the single most common mistake and it is worth being clear about:**

> A sports-pass aggregator sells access subscriptions and pays venues per check-in. **It does not buy inventory, hold stock, run a warehouse, or resell products.** There is no purchasing department to sell 3,000 cans to. **Pitching "we'd like to be listed by AYCM" marks you as someone who hasn't understood their business. Do not send them a wholesale pitch.**

**What they are worth instead:** their partner-venue directories are the densest, highest-quality free prospect list in Hungary — and better than a generic gym directory in a specific way. A venue that joined AYCM or MultiSport has already (i) negotiated a third-party commercial agreement, (ii) accepted a partner's terms, (iii) set up recurring invoicing to a corporate counterparty, and (iv) decided extra footfall is worth a margin concession. **All four are preconditions for stocking a new supplier.** A venue on both networks is doubly qualified — and it will not be spooked by a delivery note and an invoice.

⚠️ **URL warning.** `aycm.hu` was attempted in research and returned a **proxy policy block, which is not confirmation the site exists**. It is an **UNVERIFIED CANDIDATE** — find it in a browser, do not print it on anything. **No MultiSport URL is recorded anywhere in this project and none has been guessed.** Find it from Benefit Systems' own site or from any partner gym's page, where the accepted-pass logos are normally linked.

**Marketing partnership with either network: correct sequencing is to build 20–30 venue relationships first, then open with *"we're already in 30 of your partner venues."*** That conversation has leverage. Day-one has none.

### The rest of the prospect stack

Full method in `sales/26_GYM_PROSPECTS_HOWTO.md`. Priority: AYCM/MultiSport directories → **CrossFit official affiliate map (`map.crossfit.com`)** → chain *Klubjaink* pages → Google Maps by district → supplement stores → university sport centres (TF first).

**The single best predictor of a yes: does the venue already have a fridge?** A gym with a fridge has already decided to be a retailer — you are only changing what is in it.

### The one named target worth a chain-level approach

**Life1 Fitness** — the only Hungarian operator with *confirmed* in-club drink retail (the "Protein Bar", `SEARCH-VERIFIED`). Priority 10 in `26_GYM_PROSPECTS.csv`. **Approach HQ, not individual clubs.** Expect a slow answer; send it in week 1 and work independents while you wait. See `28_PREORDER_STRATEGY.md` §9 — a chain co-development deal is worth more than everything else in this file combined.

## 8. THE VISIT

| | |
|---|---|
| **When** | **Mid-morning or early afternoon.** Never 18:00 — the floor is full and nobody will talk to you |
| **Ask for** | The owner or manager, **by name** if you found it |
| **Bring** | One-page sell sheet · 2 cold cans (or the prototype, pre-production — see `28`) · the placement agreement, printed, twice · a pen |
| **Do not bring** | A deck. A laptop. A price list with wholesale prices on it |
| **Leave with** | Either a signed LOI/agreement, **or a written note of exactly why not** |
| **Reply rates** | Email to a gym manager: **~10%.** Walking in: **~70% get a conversation.** `ESTIMATE` — plan your time accordingly |

### The 30-second pitch — Hungarian, say it out loud until it is natural

> „Jó napot! [NÉV] vagyok, egy magyar fehérjeital-márkát indítok. 330 ml-es doboz, szénsavas, cukormentes, **20 gramm fehérje** — úgy iszod, mint egy üdítőt, nem úgy, mint egy turmixot.
>
> Az első gyártás 1 700 doboz, és 12–15 termet keresek, aki kirakja a hűtőbe. **Nem kell érte fizetnie előre.** Kihelyezem, 899 forint a javasolt fogyasztói ár, és **minden eladott dobozon 100 forint marad Önöknél**. Kéthetente jövök, megszámoljuk, és csak az elfogyott dobozokat számlázom. Ha 90 nap alatt nem fogy, elviszem, az én költségemen.
>
> **Van két perce?"**

### The closing question — this is the one that produces a decision

> „Ha most a kezébe adnék 24 dobozt, amiért nem fizet semmit, és minden eladott dobozon 100 forint maradna Önnél — kirakná a hűtőbe?"

> **The concession, if and only if they push back on the 100 Ft:** „Százhatvan forintig el tudok menni, de akkor az ár 899 marad — az alatt nem tudom kihelyezni." **Never move the 899 to buy the cut, and never go past 160 Ft.** Both moves put the can below cost.

A yes to that is an account. A "maybe, send me an email" is a no — treat it as one, and move on.

## 9. OBJECTION HANDLING — scripted

**Rule: answer in one or two sentences, then ask a question back.** Long answers read as anxiety.

| # | Objection (as you'll hear it) | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| **1** | „Már veszünk a BioTechUSA-tól / Scitectől." | **Category, never price.** „Ez nem fehérjeturmix és nem porkiegészítő — ez egy üdítő. Nem a polcon a versenytársuk, hanem a hűtőben, a Red Bull mellett. Más vásárlási pillanat." |
| **2** | „Drága. 899 forint sok egy dobozért." | „A Barebells ugyanebben a 330-as dobozban 890–999 forint, és az egy tejes turmix. Fehérjeporban egy adag 790 forint — és azt még el kell keverni. **Ez ugyanaz a kategória, csak kész van, hideg és szénsavas.** Egy Red Bull 549, és nulla gramm fehérje van benne." |
| **3** | „Nem akarok pénzt kötni bele." | „Nem is kell. **Nulla forint.** Csak azt fizeti ki, ami elfogyott, kéthetente. Ami nem fogy, azt elviszem." **This is the whole offer — if you hear this objection you buried the lede. Lead with it next time.** |
| **4** | „Nincs hely a hűtőben." | „Egy karton, egy polcrész. Ha két hét alatt nem mozdul, elviszem és nem beszélünk róla többet." Then offer the top shelf at eye level — ask, don't assume. |
| **5** | „Nem ismerem a márkát / még nem is létezik." | **Be honest, it converts better.** „Így van, ez az első gyártás. Ezért nem kérek Öntől pénzt. **Tizenöt termet keresek, akik elsőként kapják meg** — és Ön 90 napig az egyetlen lesz a kerületben." |
| **6** | „És ha nem fogy?" | „Akkor 90 nap múlva elviszem, az én költségemen, és Ön nem fizetett semmit. **Írásban benne van.**" Point at the clause. |
| **7** | „Van benne koffein? Fiatalok is járnak ide." | „**Nincs. Szándékosan nincs.** Az első termékben nulla koffein — bárki megihatja, nincs figyelmeztető felirat, nincs korhatár." (True and deliberate: `no caffeine SKU in batch 1`.) |
| **8** | „Laktózérzékeny tagjaink is vannak. Tejtermék?" | **Never soften this.** „Tartalmaz tejsavófehérjét, tehát **tejallergén van benne, és fel is van tüntetve a dobozon**. A fehérje fele kollagén, az tejmentes — de aki tejallergiás, annak nem való." A gym that gets a bad allergen answer will never trust you again. |
| **9** | „Küldjön egy árlistát / írjon e-mailt." | The brush-off. „Küldök. De hagyok itt két dobozt — kóstolja meg az edzőkkel. **Csütörtökön ugyanekkor visszajövök, és akkor csak annyit mondjon: igen vagy nem.**" Name the day. |
| **10** | „Kaphatnék kizárólagosságot a kerületben?" | **Yes — say yes fast, it costs nothing.** „Igen. 90 napra, írásban, a kerületben Ön az egyetlen. Utána közösen megnézzük a számokat." |
| **11** | „Harminc nap fizetési határidő." | „Nem tudok adni, az első gyártást a saját pénzemből fizetem. **De épp ezért nem is kérek semmit előre.**" |
| **12** | „Mennyit lehet ezen keresni?" | Do not oversell. „Havi 40–60 doboz reális egy ilyen méretű teremnél — az **4 000–6 000 forint** száz forintos résszel. Nem ettől lesz jobb a hónapjuk. **Attól lesz, hogy van a hűtőben valami, amit máshol nem kapnak meg — és nulla forint kockázattal.**" |
| **15** | „Csak száz forint marad rajtam? Az tizenegy százalék." | **Do not argue the percentage — reframe the risk.** „Igen, mert Ön nulla forintot tesz bele. Nincs bekészletezés, nincs bennragadt áru, nincs leírás. Ha nem fogy, elviszem. **Egy megvásárolt terméken 35–40% marad, de azt ki is kell fizetni előre, és a kockázat is az Öné.** Ha inkább megvenné, arról a második gyártás után tudunk beszélni." Then, if needed, concede to 160 Ft — **never below 899 RRP.** |
| **13** | „Kell rá visszaváltási díj / betétdíj?" | **Say nothing until the legal opinion lands.** If asked directly: „Ezt most tisztázza az ügyvédem, és amint megvan, írásban megküldöm." Never assert the DRS position to a customer before it is confirmed (`legal/10_DRS_REPONT.md`). |
| **14** | „Ki szállítja? Mennyi a minimum?" | „Én szállítom, személyesen. **Nincs minimum.** Egy karton is jó." |

### The three things that lose the deal

1. **Leading with the money.** 8,000 HUF/month is not persuasive. Novelty, zero risk and exclusivity are.
2. **Guessing an answer.** „Nem tudom, de utánanézek és holnap megírom" is a stronger answer than a confident wrong one — and in a small gym community a wrong answer travels.
3. **Leaving without a date.** Every visit ends with either a signature or a named day you come back.

## 10. WHAT TO TRACK — one row per account, per visit

| Field | Why |
|---|---|
| Date placed, cans placed, batch number | Legal traceability + your inventory |
| **Days to first reorder** ⭐ | **The single most important number in the business.** If your first ten accounts don't reorder, you don't have a distribution problem — you have a product or price problem, and adding accounts spreads the failure wider |
| Cans sold per 14-day cycle | The only real demand signal |
| Shelf price the gym actually charges | Free primary research into real willingness-to-pay. **Your floor is 899. If several gyms price ABOVE 899 and still sell, batch 2 is underpriced** — `C2_hungary_retail_pricing.md` independently derived 950–1,150 HUF for specialist retail and 1,250–1,800 for a gym bar |
| **Free cans given away, running total** | **Against the 60-can cap (§5).** Break-even dies at ~235 free cans. This is the one number that can silently kill the batch |
| Objection given, verbatim | Patterns across 20 gyms tell you what to fix in batch 2 |
| Fridge position (eye level / bottom / back) | Explains half your variance between accounts |

> **Ten accounts with a proven reorder rate are worth more than forty without one** — and they are the only evidence that will ever raise money or open a chain.

## 11. THE FREE CONCESSIONS — give these, they cost nothing

| Concession | Cost to you | When to play it |
|---|---|---|
| **90-day district exclusivity, in writing** | 0 HUF | Whenever asked, and volunteer it to your first 5 accounts |
| Free branded shelf card / fridge sticker | print cost | At signing |
| Named as a launch partner on your Instagram | 0 HUF | At signing — gyms like this more than you'd expect |
| **You collect unsold stock at your cost** | ~1 case risk | In the agreement from the start, it is the whole offer |
| A personal discount code for their coaches | margin on a few cans | To convert the coach who will actually recommend it |
| Delivery in person, any quantity, no minimum | your time | Always |

**Never concede, in this order of importance:**
1. **The 899 RRP.** It is the price the batch is funded at. At 799 with a 160 HUF cut you lose money on every can.
2. **A gym cut above 160 HUF.** Break-even is 233 HUF; 160 already leaves you only 57.89 HUF/can.
3. **A wholesale price in batch 1.** Any number you say becomes the number you are held to, and every affordable one is below cost.
4. **30-day terms.** Settlement is 14 days, on the visit.
5. **A free case.** The consignment IS the free trial.
