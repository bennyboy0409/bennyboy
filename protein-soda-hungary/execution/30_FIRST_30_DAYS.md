# 30 — THE FIRST 30 DAYS, DAY BY DAY

**Total spend across 30 days: ~€350.** No cans are ordered in this window.
By Day 30 you will know whether to spend €2,300 or walk away.

Legend: 🔴 blocking · 🟡 important · 🟢 useful

## WEEK 1 — DECIDE WHO OWNS IT, AND START THE CLOCK ON SUPPLIERS

### DAY 1 🔴 — The conversation with your father
- **Action:** explain the plan and ask two things: (a) will he be the legal operator, (b) will he commit €2,000 *if* Day 30 evidence justifies it
- **Show him:** `02_EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md`
- **Cost:** €0
- **Why it blocks everything:** you cannot be an *egyéni vállalkozó* or an *ügyvezető* under 18 (`Ptk. 3:22 § (1)` — quoted directly). No consent form fixes this. An adult must sign.
- **Outcome needed:** a yes/no on being the legal operator. The €2,000 can stay conditional
- **If no:** stop. Find another adult (mother, older sibling over 18, family friend) or shelve until you turn 18

### DAY 2 🔴 — Send the co-packer email to all five
- **Action:** `31_EMAILS_ENGLISH.md` §1 → Opre' (`sales@oprelabel.com`), Bubee (`info@bubee.eu`), Sajátital (`sajatital.hu@gmail.com`), MONYO, Private Label Drink
- **Cost:** €0 · **Time:** 30 minutes
- **Outcome needed:** MOQ, price/can, setup fees, whey/collagen capability, **plant location**
- **Note:** expect 2 of 5 to reply. That is normal

### DAY 3 🟡 — Order the benchmark products
- **Action:** buy Myprotein **Clear Protein Whey & Collagen** (`myprotein.hu`) — your exact architecture, sold in Hungary. Plus a NOCCO and any protein RTD
- **Cost:** ~€25
- **Outcome needed:** know what your product tastes like before you commission it

### DAY 4 🟡 — Send ingredient enquiries
- **Action:** `31_EMAILS_ENGLISH.md` §2 to collagen and whey suppliers named in `manufacturing/16_INGREDIENT_SUPPLIERS.csv`
- **Cost:** €0
- **Critical question:** *availability*, not just price. European whey producers were reported sold out in April 2026
- **If whey is unobtainable:** switch to collagen-only and re-cost. This is a real possibility — plan for it

### DAY 5 🔴 — Engage the food lawyer
- **Action:** send `32_EMAILS_HUNGARIAN.md` §7 to 2–3 Hungarian food-law practices
- **Cost:** €0 to enquire, €150–400 to instruct
- **The three questions:** DRS scope + the ~5,000 exemption · ÁFA 27% or 18% · NETA scope
- **Outcome needed:** a written opinion you can show suppliers and your accountant

### DAY 6–7 🟢 — Build the one-page sell sheet
- **Action:** one page: product shot mockup, 20 g protein / zero sugar / ~80 kcal, the price ladder vs Red Bull and HELL, the consignment offer, your phone number
- **Tool:** Canva or Figma, free
- **Cost:** €0
- **Outcome needed:** something to hand a gym manager. It does not need to be beautiful

## WEEK 2 — WALK INTO GYMS

### DAY 8 🟡 — Build the prospect list
- **Action:** open `sales/26_GYM_PROSPECTS.csv` and fill it yourself using Google Maps. Target **60 gyms** in your city
- **Cost:** €0 · **Time:** 2–3 hours
- **Why you and not the research:** contact verification required opening pages the research environment could not reach. You can do it faster and more accurately than any automated method

### DAY 9–10 🔴 — Visit 10 gyms IN PERSON
- **Action:** walk in. Ask for the manager. Use the script in `sales/27_GYM_SALES_STRATEGY.md`
- **Cost:** transport only
- **Ask exactly this:** *"If I brought you a 330 ml can with 20 g protein and zero sugar, that you paid nothing for upfront and kept 160 Ft on every one sold — would you put it in your fridge?"*
- **Outcome needed:** a yes/no from 10 humans. Write down the objections verbatim
- 🔴 **This is the single most valuable activity in the 30 days.** Ten in-person conversations tell you more than a hundred emails

### DAY 11 🟡 — Photograph three gym fridges
- **Action:** note every drink and its price
- **Cost:** €0
- **Why:** gym fridge pricing could not be researched — it is a genuine gap. This is free primary data and it will be more accurate than anything in `finance/22_PRICING.md`

### DAY 12 🟡 — Chase non-responding co-packers
- **Action:** `31_EMAILS_ENGLISH.md` §9. Reduce your ask to **one number**
- **Cost:** €0

### DAY 13–14 🟢 — Taste the benchmarks properly
- **Action:** drink them cold, in a gym context, after training. Write down what you like and what you would change
- **Outcome needed:** a flavour brief you can give a co-packer

## WEEK 3 — CONVERT INTEREST INTO SIGNATURES

### DAY 15 🔴 — Register the business
- **Action:** whichever route Day 1 settled. Cheapest: add TEÁOR **4634** + **4725** to an existing family company
- **Portal (changed 2026-02-19):** `https://ugyfelportal.nav.gov.hu/vallalkozoi-ugyseged/main`
- **Cost:** €0–165 + 5,000 HUF chamber contribution
- ⚠️ **Do NOT register a full-time egyéni vállalkozó** — 101,682 HUF/month in contributions at zero revenue

### DAY 16 🔴 — Food business registration + FELIR
- **Action:** register the food business activity with NÉBIH / the district office
- **Cost:** €0 · **Must be done before first sale**

### DAY 17–19 🔴 — Collect Letters of Intent
- **Action:** go back to every gym that said yes. Get the LOI in `32_EMAILS_HUNGARIAN.md` §8 signed
- **Cost:** €0
- **Target: 8+ signed LOIs covering ~1,000 cans (60% of batch 1)**
- 🔴 **This is the go/no-go gate.** The LOI is free and non-binding — that is exactly why a refusal to sign is such useful information

### DAY 20 🟡 — Compare the co-packer quotes
- **Action:** fill in `manufacturing/12_COPACKERS.csv` with real numbers. Recompute landed COGS
- **Outcome needed:** know your actual cost per can, not the estimate

### DAY 21 🟢 — Instagram exists
- **Action:** claim the handle, post the product concept, follow 200 local gym accounts
- **Cost:** €0. Do not buy ads

## WEEK 4 — DECIDE

### DAY 22 🟡 — The legal opinion lands
- **Action:** read it. Update `legal/10_DRS_REPONT.md` and `finance/22_PRICING.md` with the confirmed answers
- **If ÁFA is 18% not 27%:** you just gained ~57 HUF/can. Recompute everything
- **If the 5,000 exemption is not real:** stop and re-plan the artwork before ordering

### DAY 23–24 🟡 — Negotiate
- **Action:** work `33_NEGOTIATION_PLAN.md` against your best quote
- **Priorities in order:** lower MOQ → 30% deposit not 50% → turnkey ingredients → sleeves included → DDP Budapest

### DAY 25 🟢 — Second-source the risk
- **Action:** if whey is unavailable or dairy approval is required, get a collagen-only quote as your fallback
- **Cost:** €0

### DAY 26–27 🟡 — Finalise artwork
- **Action:** label text from `legal/09_LABEL_COMPLIANCE.md`, laid out in Canva/Figma against the co-packer's dieline
- ⚠️ **No DRS mark if staying under 5,000 units. Milk allergen MUST be emphasised** (`Reg. 1169/2011 Art. 21`)

### DAY 28 🔴 — THE DECISION
Order **only if all four are true:**

- [ ] An adult legal entity exists and is registered
- [ ] A written co-packer quote at **≤550 HUF/can landed** for ~1,700 cans
- [ ] **8+ signed LOIs** covering ~1,000 cans
- [ ] The legal opinion confirms the DRS position

**All four true →** proceed to Day 29.
**Any false →** do not order. Fix it, or stop. **Stopping here costs you €350 and no debt.**

### DAY 29 🔴 — Place the order
- **Action:** confirm, pay the deposit (target 30%, expect 50%)
- **Cost:** €700–1,150
- **Get in writing:** quantity, spec, price, lead time, CoA per batch, delivery terms

### DAY 30 🟢 — Set up for arrival
- **Action:** clear ~1 m² of dry, ambient storage. Start the traceability log (batch number, quantity, date, destination). Book delivery slots with your LOI gyms
- **Cost:** €0

## WHAT DAY 30 LOOKS LIKE IN EACH CASE

| Scenario | Spent | Position |
|---|---|---|
| **Everything worked** | ~€1,500 (€350 + deposit) | 1,700 cans in production, 8 gyms waiting |
| **Gyms said no** | ~€350 | You learned it doesn't sell **before** buying stock. Cheapest possible failure |
| **No co-packer under 5,000 MOQ** | ~€350 | Pivot to the powder SKU (~1,000 sachets, €700–1,500) and revisit cans later |
| **Father said no** | ~€25 | Wait until 18, or find another adult |

> **Three of these four outcomes are good.** Only one involves losing money, and it is the one where you find out cheaply that the market doesn't want it — which is worth far more than €350.
