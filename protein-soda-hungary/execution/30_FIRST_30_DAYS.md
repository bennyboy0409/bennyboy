**Total spend across 30 days: ~€430** (itemised at the bottom of this file — the header and the table used to disagree; €430 is the correct one). No cans are ordered in this window.

## WEEK 1 — DECIDE WHO OWNS IT, AND START THE CLOCK ON SUPPLIERS

### DAY 1 🔴 — The conversation with your father
- **Action:** explain the plan and ask **three** things: (a) will he be the legal operator, (b) **does an adult-owned family company already exist, and what is its ÁFA status?**, (c) will he commit €2,000 *if* Day 30 evidence justifies it
- **Show him:** `02_EXECUTIVE_SUMMARY.md`
- **Cost:** €0
- **Why it blocks everything:** you cannot be an *egyéni vállalkozó* or an *ügyvezető* under 18 (`Ptk. 3:22 § (1)` · `LIKELY / SEARCH-VERIFIED — njt.hu was blocked, wording corroborated across practitioner sources`). No consent form fixes this. An adult must sign.
- 🔴 **Why (b) is not a detail:** every budget in this project uses an **€80 business-setup line that assumes a family company already exists.** If it does not, the real fallback is a secondary-occupation egyéni vállalkozó at **~185,000 HUF/yr (€510)** `ESTIMATE`, which takes setup/legal from €440 to ~€950 and total capital need to ~€3,500. **Ask on Day 1, not Day 15.**
- **Outcome needed:** a yes/no on being the legal operator, plus the company answer. The €2,000 can stay conditional
- **If no:** stop. Find another adult (mother, older sibling over 18, family friend) or shelve until you turn 18

### DAY 1 (same day) 🔴 — Thirty minutes that could kill the whole plan for free
- **Action:** search German, Polish and UK retailer and supplement sites for a **complete-protein (18–25 g) carbonated can**. Try dm.de, rossmann.pl, allegro.pl, holland&barrett, bodybuilding retailers, and the obvious brand names
- **Cost:** €0 · **Time:** 30 minutes
- **Why:** the entire strategic premise of this project is *"the European shelf is empty."* **No dedicated UK, German or Polish competitor sweep was ever completed** — that claim is absence of evidence, not proof of absence
- **If you find one shipping:** the speed-and-distribution premise is dead. **Re-plan before spending anything.** That is a good outcome on Day 1 and a catastrophic one on Day 60

The Day-19 LOI gate depends entirely on a list that does not yet exist. Build it first so a slip does not eat the gate

### DAY 2 🔴 — Register the business, then start the FELIR clock. **THIS IS THE LONG POLE**
- **Action:** whichever route Day 1 settled — cheapest is adding TEÁOR **4634** + **4725** to an existing adult-owned family company. **Then** notify the food business activity to NÉBIH / the district office and obtain a FELIR identifier
- **Portal:** `https://ugyfelportal.nav.gov.hu/vallalkozoi-ugyseged/main` — the NAV Ügyfélportál went live **2026-02-19**; the old nyilvántartó.hu Webes Ügysegéd stopped accepting new applications **2026-03-01**
- **Cost:** €0–165 + 5,000 HUF chamber contribution · **FELIR lead time: 2–4 weeks**
- 🔴 **Why this moved from Day 15 to Day 2:** the food-business notification is the **long pole on the whole critical path**, and it cannot be filed until an adult legal entity exists. Filing it in Week 4 pushes first sale into 2027. **Realistic first-sale window is mid-November to December 2026, and only if this goes out in Week 1**
- **Send the same week:** the NÉBIH five-question letter (`31_EMAILS_ENGLISH.md` §6 / `32_EMAILS_HUNGARIAN.md` §6). **Allow 2–6 weeks for a reply**

### DAY 3 🟡 — Order the benchmark products
- **Action:** buy Myprotein **Clear Protein Whey & Collagen** (`myprotein.hu`) — your exact architecture, sold in Hungary. Plus a NOCCO and any protein RTD
- **Cost:** ~€25
- **Outcome needed:** know what your product tastes like before you commission it

### DAY 4 🟡 — Send ingredient enquiries
- **Action:** `31_EMAILS_ENGLISH.md` §2 to collagen and whey suppliers named in `manufacturing/16_INGREDIENT_SUPPLIERS.csv`
- **Cost:** €0
- **Critical question:** *availability*, not just price. European whey producers were reported sold out in April 2026
- 🔴 **Specify the grade in writing: CLEAR / ACID-STABLE whey isolate, certified clear at pH 3.0–3.5. NOT standard WPI90.** Commodity whey will haze at pH 3.2 — it is not a cheaper substitute, it is a different product. The clear grade is budgeted at **163.90 HUF/can** against 131.08 for commodity, and that single line swings the batch by **±50 HUF/can**. Also ask what the **25 kg small-lot uplift** is over index (budget +10–25%) `QUOTE REQUIRED`
- **If whey is unobtainable:** switch to collagen-only and re-cost. Swapping the 10 g of clear whey for collagen saves **~99 HUF/can**. This is a real possibility — plan for it

### DAY 5 🔴 — Engage the food lawyer
- **Action:** send `32_EMAILS_HUNGARIAN.md` §7 to 2–3 Hungarian food-law practices
- **Cost:** €0 to enquire, €150–400 to instruct
- **The FIVE questions — all five, in one brief:**
  1. **DRS scope** + is the ~5,000-unit exemption real, automatic or notified, and unchanged for 2026?
  2. **Reg. 853/2004** — does a whey-containing beverage need a dairy-approved filling plant? *(This one decides your entire co-packer pool.)*
  3. **ÁFA** 27% or 18%?
  4. **Free amino acids** — may free L-leucine and L-tryptophan lawfully be added to a **beverage** under Reg. (EC) 1925/2006? *(This one blocks raw-material purchase and artwork.)*
  5. **NETA** scope, with and without caffeine — and can the front-of-pack "20 g protein" claim rest on a recipe calculation, or does it need a finished-can nitrogen analysis?
- ⚠️ **Do not let the lawyer scope this down to three.** Questions 2 and 4 are the ones that can stop the project, and they are the two most often left out
- **Outcome needed:** a written opinion you can show suppliers and your accountant

### DAY 6–7 🟢 — Build the one-page sell sheet
- **Action:** one page: product shot mockup, 20 g protein / zero sugar / ~80 kcal, the price ladder vs Red Bull and HELL, the consignment offer at **899 Ft with 100 Ft to the gym**, your phone number
- ⚠️ **Do not put a per-gram-of-protein price on it.** At 899 Ft that is 45 HUF per gram of protein, which is *more* expensive than a protein bar (25–35) and than the cheap half of protein RTDs. You win on **protein per can versus energy drinks** and on **format versus bars** — sell those, not the per-gram number
- ⚠️ **No wholesale price list. No leucine claim on the front.** Neither goes on paper in batch 1
- **Tool:** Canva or Figma, free
- **Cost:** €0
- **Outcome needed:** something to hand a gym manager. It does not need to be beautiful

### DAY 9–10 🔴 — Visit 10 gyms IN PERSON
- **Action:** walk in. Ask for the manager. Use the script in `sales/27_GYM_SALES_STRATEGY.md`
- **Cost:** transport only
- **Ask exactly this:** *"If I brought you a 330 ml can with 20 g protein and zero sugar, that you paid nothing for upfront and kept 100 Ft on every one sold at 899 Ft — would you put it in your fridge?"*
- 💰 **Know your floor before you walk in.** At a 524 HUF cost: **899 with the gym keeping 100 Ft earns you +105 HUF/can · 899 with the gym keeping 160 Ft earns +58 HUF/can · 799 with the gym keeping 160 Ft LOSES 21 HUF/can.** Open at 100 Ft. **160 Ft is the absolute ceiling of what batch 1 can carry, and only at 899.** Never combine 799 with a 160 Ft cut
- **Outcome needed:** a yes/no from 10 humans. Write down the objections verbatim
- 🔴 **This is the single most valuable activity in the 30 days.** Ten in-person conversations tell you more than a hundred emails

### DAY 17–19 🔴 — Collect Letters of Intent
- **Action:** go back to every gym that said yes. Get the LOI in `32_EMAILS_HUNGARIAN.md` §8 signed — **at 899 Ft, gym keeps 100–160 Ft**
- **Cost:** €0
- **Target: 8+ signed LOIs covering ~1,000 cans (61% of the 1,640 sellable)**

- ⚠️ **Contingency, decided now so it is not decided under pressure:** if by Day 19 you have fewer than **30 prospect rows** or fewer than **10 in-person visits** done, **extend the gate by a week. Do not lower the LOI threshold to make the date.** The gate is the only thing standing between you and 1,700 unsold cans
- 🧮 **And be honest about what the LOIs do and do not cover.** 8–15 gym accounts realistically absorb ~1,000–1,100 cans inside the selling window. **The remaining ~550–650 cans you hand-sell yourself** — roughly 7 cans a day for 90 days, each needing an individual receipt. That workload is real, it is unpaid, and it is not costed anywhere in this plan

### DAY 20 🟡 — Compare the co-packer quotes
- **Action:** fill in `manufacturing/12_COPACKERS.csv` with real numbers. Recompute landed COGS
- **Outcome needed:** know your actual cost per can, not the estimate
- 🧮 **Run two tests on every quote, not one:** (a) **per-can** — is landed cost ≤550 HUF? (b) **affordability** — is *quoted cost × MOQ* inside your ~€2,300 inventory budget? **A quote can pass (a) and fail (b).** At 524 HUF your ceiling is ~1,590 cans; a 3,000-can MOQ is €4,331 and a 5,000-can MOQ is €7,218
- **Also recompute the RRP if COGS moves.** Every price in this plan is built on 524 HUF. At 620 HUF (the top of the range) direct-at-899 contribution falls to +88 HUF/can and a 160 Ft gym cut goes negative

Public transport with a hand truck carries **4 cases (96 cans) maximum** and cannot move the initial placement of ~68 cases

## WEEK 4 — DECIDE

### DAY 22 🟡 — The legal opinion lands
- **Action:** read it. Update `legal/10_DRS_REPONT.md` and `finance/22_PRICING.md` with the confirmed answers
- **If ÁFA is 18% not 27%:** you gain **~48 HUF/can**, not 57 `DERIVED: 899÷1.18 − 899÷1.27 = 761.86 − 707.87 = 53.99 at the 899 RRP; 48.0 at 799`. ⚠️ **Treat this as a low-probability branch. Ask, but do not plan against it** — `legal/07_HUNGARY_BUSINESS_SETUP.md` is the deeper file and concludes a clear carbonated soft drink lands at 27%
- **If free amino acids may NOT be added to a beverage:** drop L-leucine and L-tryptophan, save 10.31 HUF/can, drop any leucine copy, and **re-check the label before artwork**
- **If Reg. 853/2004 dairy approval IS required:** most can fillers are disqualified. Go to the collagen-only fallback quote from Day 25
- **If the 5,000 exemption is not real:** stop and re-plan the artwork before ordering

### DAY 23–24 🟡 — Negotiate
- **Action:** work `33_NEGOTIATION_PLAN.md` against your best quote
- **Priorities in order:** lower MOQ → 30% deposit not 50% → turnkey ingredients → sleeves included → DDP Budapest

### DAY 25 🟢 — Second-source the risk
- **Action:** if whey is unavailable or dairy approval is required, get a collagen-only quote as your fallback
- **Cost:** €0

### DAY 26–27 🟡 — Finalise artwork
- **Action:** label text from `legal/09_LABEL_COMPLIANCE.md`, laid out against the co-packer's dieline
- ⚠️ **No DRS mark if staying under the threshold. Milk allergen MUST be emphasised** (`Reg. 1169/2011 Art. 21`)
- 🛑 **Two free checks that must happen BEFORE artwork is signed off, because a sleeve run is irreversible:**
  - **Trademark sweep of the chosen name** — free TMview + EUIPO eSearch + SZTNH, class 32 (and 5/29/30). **Zero trademark searches were performed anywhere in this project**, and class 32 is one of the most crowded classes that exists
  - **Patent check** — 30 minutes with an EP/HU patent attorney on **EP2001312B1**, which appears to cover pH 2–3.4 carbonated whey-isolate beverages and runs to ~9 March 2027. Its claim scope and Hungarian validation are **UNVERIFIED**, and the number itself was found by web search, not in the research record. **Fee: UNKNOWN — QUOTE REQUIRED.** It is live during batch 1, which is why it cannot wait for batch 2
- ⚠️ **Do not sign off artwork until the free-amino-acid answer is in** (Day 22). If it lands the wrong way, the ingredient list changes

### DAY 28 🔴 — THE DECISION
Order **only if all five are true:**

- [ ] An adult legal entity exists and is registered
- [ ] A written co-packer quote at **≤550 HUF/can landed** for ~1,700 cans, **AND the batch total fits inside your €2,300 inventory budget.** Check both. A quote can pass the per-can test and still be unaffordable
- [ ] **Cans per pallet answered in writing**, and a part-pallet fill confirmed if a pallet is larger than ~1,700 cans. **Maximum acceptable order = your capital ÷ the quoted landed cost, and in no case above 4,000 cans in year one**
- [ ] **8+ signed LOIs** covering ~1,000 cans
- [ ] The legal opinion answers all five questions, including DRS and the amino acids

**All five true →** proceed to Day 29.
**Any false →** do not order. Fix it, or stop. **Stopping here costs you €430 and no debt.**

### DAY 29 🔴 — Place the order
- **Action:** confirm, pay the deposit (target 30%, expect 50%)
- **Cost:** **€690–1,150** (30–50% of the ~€2,300 inventory invoice)
- **Get in writing:** quantity, spec, price, lead time, CoA per batch, delivery terms, **and the thermal process plus the resulting AMBIENT shelf life.** If the answer is cold-fill with no pasteurisation and no challenge test, the real shelf life may be days-to-weeks in a fridge and the gym-fridge model does not work. **That answer is a no-go condition, not a detail**

### DAY 30 🟢 — Set up for arrival
- **Action:** clear ~1 m² of dry, ambient storage. Start the traceability log (batch number, quantity, date, destination). Book delivery slots with your LOI gyms
- **Cost:** €0
